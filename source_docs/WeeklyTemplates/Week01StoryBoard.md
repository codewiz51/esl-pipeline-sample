WEEK 01 – STORY BOARD (SAMPLE: invented characters and setting)

WEEKLY VERB FOCUS:
Make – Nora makes a latte for a customer. – Nora prepara un café con leche para una clienta. (hacer / preparar)
Do – I do the dishes after work. – Lavo los platos después del trabajo. (hacer)
Take – She takes the order to table four. – Ella lleva el pedido a la mesa cuatro. (llevar)
(Make y do se traducen casi siempre como "hacer", pero no se usan igual. Make es crear o preparar algo: make coffee, make a mistake. Do es una actividad o una tarea: do the dishes, do homework.)
(Los tres verbos son irregulares en pasado: make → made, do → did, take → took. No llevan -ed.)
(Este storyboard es de ejemplo: los personajes y el café son inventados.)

# === WEEK NOTES ===
Setting: the Corner Bean Café, a small coffee shop on a busy street. Hours: Monday to Friday 7 a.m. to 3 p.m.; Saturday 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.; Sunday closed. Customers are regulars and people on their way to work. The owner is Sam Ellis. Bea works the counter with Nora, Omar cooks, and Wren is the cashier.
Cast for this week: Lila, Hana and Pia appear on Monday morning. Tuesday, Thursday and Friday are café days, and every conversation is a café or work conversation. Wednesday is errands after work. On Saturday, Nora spends the day with Lila, Hana and Pia, and Kit calls from the café. On Sunday, Nora is at home with Lila.
Nora's situation: this is her third full-time week at the café. She is calmer now, and she knows what to do when an order goes wrong.
Scope of work: Nora takes orders, makes drinks, keeps the counter clean and tells Bea or Sam when something is wrong. She never handles the money or the bank deposit.
# === END WEEK NOTES ===

# MONDAY STORYBOARD

Introduce "make" – "hacer / preparar"
Examples:
I make a latte for the first customer. – Preparo un café con leche para el primer cliente.
Mr. Reed makes a mistake with his order. – El señor Reed se equivoca con su pedido.
I made breakfast for my daughter. – Le preparé el desayuno a mi hija.
What do you make for dinner? – ¿Qué preparas de cena?

# === VOCAB MONDAY ===

a little behind – un poco atrasada
let's get the ball rolling – vamos a empezar / manos a la obra
regular – cliente habitual
rush – la hora pico
hang tight / please wait – espere un momentico / favor de esperar (casual / formal)

# === END VOCAB MONDAY ===

1 - Monday is not Nora's carpool day. Hana drives Lila and Pia to school.
2 - Nora feels a little behind this morning. She did not sleep well, but she arrives at the café at 6:45.
3 - She walks in and says, "Good morning! Let's get the ball rolling." It is a phrase she learned from Bea.
4 - At 7:15, the morning rush starts. A regular, Mr. Reed, orders a large latte with oat milk.
5 - Nora makes the latte. She is busy, so she uses the wrong milk. Mr. Reed smiles and says, "Hang tight, I think that is not oat milk."
6 - Nora feels embarrassed. She stays calm, says, "Sorry about that," and makes a new latte.
7 - She tells Bea about the mistake. Bea says, "It happens to everyone."
8 - The line is long again. Nora smiles and says, "Okay, who's next?"

# TUESDAY STORYBOARD

Introduce "do" – contrast with "make"
Examples:
Omar does the dishes after the rush. – Omar lava los platos después de la hora pico.
I do my homework at night. – Hago mi tarea por la noche.
Nora did the cleaning on Tuesday. – Nora hizo la limpieza el martes.
She makes tea. – Ella prepara té.

# === VOCAB TUESDAY ===

dishes – los platos
cleaning – la limpieza
cash register – la caja registradora
no worries – no se preocupe / tranquilo
slow day – un día tranquilo

# === END VOCAB TUESDAY ===

1 - Nora arrives with her own coffee. She had a long night with Lila's homework.
2 - The café is quiet, so Omar does the dishes and Nora does the cleaning.
3 - Wren comes to the counter and says, "I made a mistake at the cash register. A customer paid twice."
4 - Nora says, "No worries. Tell Sam, and he will fix it."
5 - Sam fixes the problem in two minutes. He makes a note in a small book: "Double payment, fixed."
6 - Then Nora calls out, "Who's up next?"

# WEDNESDAY STORYBOARD

Light review of "make" and "do"
Examples:
I make a list before I go shopping. – Hago una lista antes de ir de compras.
I do the shopping on Wednesday. – Hago las compras los miércoles.

# === VOCAB WEDNESDAY ===

ATM – cajero automático
groceries – la compra / los mandados
next in line / who's up next – el próximo / ¿quién sigue? (formal / casual)
receipt – recibo

# === END VOCAB WEDNESDAY ===

1 - After work, Nora stops at the ATM. Then she goes to the grocery store for groceries.
2 - Lila calls. She tells her mom, "I need poster board for my science project. It is due Friday."
3 - The line at the store is long. Nora feels tired, and her feet hurt.
4 - The cashier calls out, "Next in line!" Then she says, "Who's up next?" Nora smiles. She says the same thing at the café.
5 - Nora checks her receipt in the car. She has groceries and poster board, and she feels ready for Thursday.

# THURSDAY STORYBOARD

Introduce "take" – light reinforcement of "make" and "do"
Contrast: "bring" = toward here (traer); "take" = to another place (llevar).
Light past and future tenses are fine when the story needs it.
Examples:
Nora takes the order to table four. – Nora lleva el pedido a la mesa cuatro.
I take the bus to work on Thursday. – Tomo el autobús al trabajo los jueves.
Teo took the boxes to the kitchen. – Teo llevó las cajas a la cocina.
Can you take this plate to Omar? – ¿Me puedes llevar este plato a Omar?
Please bring me a clean cup. – Tráeme una taza limpia, por favor. (bring, not take)

# === VOCAB THURSDAY ===

espresso machine – máquina de espresso
old-school – a la antigua
delivery – entrega
out of stock – agotado
back door – puerta trasera
a quick fix – una solución rápida

# === END VOCAB THURSDAY ===

1 - Thursday morning, the espresso machine has a blinking red light. The water tank is empty and the pump will not start.
2 - Nora sighs and says, "Okay, old-school today." She makes drip coffee in the big pot.
3 - Teo arrives at the back door with the bakery delivery. He takes the boxes to the kitchen and does not say much. Nora says, "Thanks, Teo," and he smiles.
4 - Bea tells Nora that the bakery is out of stock of the blueberry muffins. Nora makes a small sign for the counter.
5 - A customer asks, "Where is my latte?" Nora says, "The machine is down, but I can make you a drip coffee. It is a quick fix."
6 - The customer laughs and says, "Old-school is fine."
7 - Nora thinks about her oldest brother, Luis, in the family's home country. He and his wife live in a nice house near where they grew up. He calls on Sundays.
8 - Sam says, "Good idea with the sign."

# FRIDAY STORYBOARD

Reinforce "make," "do," and "take"
Heavier use of future, present, past tenses are permitted.
Examples: Create 2 examples for "take" and one each for "make" and "do."
A mother takes her little boy to the park. – Una mamá lleva a su niño al parque.
Nora made a small cup of hot chocolate for the boy. – Nora le preparó una tacita de chocolate caliente al niño.
The boy does a drawing at the table. – El niño hace un dibujo en la mesa.
Nora took the drawing to the counter right away. – Nora llevó el dibujo al mostrador enseguida.

# === VOCAB FRIDAY ===

drawing – dibujo
to spill – derramar
napkin – servilleta
birthday cake – pastel de cumpleaños
we're going to take care of it – nosotros nos encargamos

# === END VOCAB FRIDAY ===

1. On Friday afternoon, a mother rushes in with her little boy, Max. He is four years old.
2. Max wants a hot chocolate. His mother orders one small cup and a muffin.
3. Nora makes the hot chocolate and takes it to the table. Max does a drawing with the crayons she gives him.
4. Max spills the cup. The chocolate goes on the table and on the floor.
5. The mother is embarrassed. Nora says in a calm voice, "We're going to take care of it."
6. She brings a napkin and a towel and does the cleaning in one minute.
7. Max gives Nora his drawing. It is a picture of a big cup of chocolate.
8. Nora tapes it on the wall behind the counter. She feels proud, and she makes Max a new hot chocolate.

# SATURDAY STORYBOARD

Light reinforcement of "make," "do," and "take"
Future, present and past tenses are permitted.
Examples: Create one example each for "make," "do," and "take."
Lila makes pancakes this morning. – Lila hace panqueques esta mañana.
Hana does yoga in the park. – Hana hace yoga en el parque.
Nora takes the girls to the library. – Nora lleva a las niñas a la biblioteca.

# === VOCAB SATURDAY ===

two peas in a pod – dos gotas de agua
pancakes – panqueques
call in sick – reportarse enfermo / enferma
cover a shift – cubrir un turno
I already have plans – ya tengo planes

# === END VOCAB SATURDAY ===

1. Saturday morning is quiet. Nora stays under the warm blankets. Lila comes in, climbs into the bed, and says, "I made pancakes, but they are a little burned."
2. Nora laughs. "We are two peas in a pod."
3. Someone knocks on the door. It is Hana and Pia with a bag of fresh bread. Hana says, "Pia woke me up early, so we took a walk to the bakery."
4. They plan a trip to the library at 11:00.
5. Then the phone rings. It is Kit from the café: "Ivy called in sick for tomorrow. Can you cover her shift?"
6. Nora knows Ivy often calls in sick on weekends. She takes a deep breath and says, "I'm sorry. I already have plans. Tomorrow is my day with Lila."
7. She feels a little guilty, but she shakes it off. She covers for other people all the time.
8. At the library, Lila and Pia do a puzzle and read for an hour. By the end of the day, Nora feels full of peace.

# SUNDAY STORYBOARD

Use "make," "do," and "take"
Examples:
Present
Nora makes coffee this morning. – Nora prepara café esta mañana.
Lila does her reading on the sofa. – Lila hace su lectura en el sofá.
Lila takes her mom a second cup of coffee. – Lila le lleva a su mamá otra taza de café.
Past
Nora made a new latte on Monday. – Nora preparó un café con leche nuevo el lunes.
Omar did the dishes on Tuesday. – Omar lavó los platos el martes.
Teo took the boxes to the kitchen. – Teo llevó las cajas a la cocina.
Future
Nora will make drip coffee again if the machine breaks. – Nora va a preparar café filtrado otra vez si la máquina se rompe.
She will do the cleaning on Monday. – Ella va a hacer la limpieza el lunes.
Hana will take the girls to school. – Hana va a llevar a las niñas a la escuela.

# === VOCAB SUNDAY ===

regular – cliente habitual
dishes – los platos
espresso machine – máquina de espresso
call in sick – reportarse enfermo / enferma
a little behind – un poco atrasada

# === END VOCAB SUNDAY ===

1 - Sunday morning, there is no alarm. Nora drinks coffee on the porch and remembers her week.
2 - Monday: Nora made the wrong latte for Mr. Reed. She stayed calm and told Bea.
3 - Tuesday: The café was quiet, and Omar did the dishes.
4 - Thursday: The espresso machine was down, so Nora made drip coffee. Teo took the boxes to the kitchen.
5 - Friday: A little boy spilled his hot chocolate. Nora did the cleaning and made a new one.
6 - Saturday: Ivy called in sick again, and Nora said no. She felt a little guilty, but not for long.
7 - Lila brings her a second cup of coffee. Nora feels calm. She chose her daughter, and it was the right choice.
