# MONDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. a little behind – un poco atrasada
2. let's get the ball rolling – vamos a empezar / manos a la obra
3. regular – cliente habitual
4. rush – la hora pico

## Grammar Focus

Nora uses "grabbed" to show she took the wrong milk, and "made" to show she finished the new latte. She moves through the morning one finished action at a time.

## Mini Story

1. Nora felt a little behind because she did not sleep well.
2. Hana drove Lila and Pia to school that day.
3. Nora arrived at the café and put on her apron.
4. Mr. Reed ordered a large latte with oat milk.
5. Nora grabbed regular milk by mistake, and he said "hang tight."
6. She made a new latte and said, "Who's next?"

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Nora se siente un poco atrasada porque no durmió bien.
2. El cliente habitual pide un latte grande con leche de avena.

English → Spanish

1. The regular says "hang tight" because the milk is not oat milk.

## Student Questions

1. What did Nora do after she made the wrong latte? (quedarse en calma)
2. Do you ever make a mistake at work or school? (respirar hondo)

# TUESDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. dishes – los platos
2. cleaning – la limpieza
3. cash register – la caja registradora
4. no worries – no se preocupe / tranquilo

## Grammar Focus

Nora uses "will" when she tells Wren that Sam will fix the problem. She gives Wren a clear answer, and Wren can stop worrying.

## Mini Story

1. Nora arrives at the café with her own coffee.
2. Omar does the dishes, and Nora does the cleaning.
3. Wren says a customer paid twice at the cash register.
4. Nora says "no worries" and tells Wren to call Sam.
5. Sam fixes the problem in two minutes and makes a note.
6. The slow day feels almost nice for Nora.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Omar lava los platos y Nora hace la limpieza.
2. Wren dice que un cliente pagó dos veces en la caja registradora.

English → Spanish

1. Nora says "no worries" and tells Wren to call Sam.

## Student Questions

1. What did Sam do after Wren told him about the double payment? (hacer una nota)
2. Do you have a slow day at work or school sometimes? (sentirse tranquilo)

# SUNDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. regular – cliente habitual
2. dishes – los platos
3. espresso machine – máquina de espresso
4. call in sick – reportarse enfermo / enferma

## Grammar Focus

Nora uses "made" to remember the wrong latte on Monday and "was" for the broken machine on Thursday. She closes each memory and feels calm with her second cup.

## Mini Story

1. Nora drinks coffee on the porch and remembers her week.
2. On Monday, she made the wrong latte for a regular.
3. On Thursday, the espresso machine was down, and she made drip coffee.
4. On Saturday, Ivy called in sick, and Nora said no.
5. Lila brings her a second cup, and Nora feels calm.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Nora bebe café en la terraza y recuerda su semana.
2. El sábado, Ivy se reportó enferma y Nora dijo que no.

English → Spanish

1. Lila brings Nora a second cup and she feels calm.

## Student Questions

1. What did Nora remember from Monday? (latte incorrecto)
2. Do you ever sit and think about your week? (café tranquilo)
