# MONDAY

## Warmup

1. I ___ a cup of coffee before I go to work. (preparo)
2. When the morning ___ starts, the café is very busy. (hora pico)
3. I feel ___ when I do not sleep well at night. (un poco atrasada)

## Vocabulary

1. a little behind – un poco atrasada
2. let's get the ball rolling – vamos a empezar / manos a la obra
3. regular – cliente habitual
4. rush – la hora pico
5. hang tight / please wait – espere un momentico / favor de esperar (casual / formal)

## Story

**English:**

Monday morning, Nora feels a little behind. She did not sleep well, and the house was cold. Hana drives Lila and Pia to school today, so Nora walks to the café. She arrives at 6:45, puts on her apron, and says, "Good morning! Let's get the ball rolling." It is a phrase she learned from Bea, and it still sounds funny in her own mouth.

At 7:15, the rush starts. The bell above the door rings fast, and coffee smell fills the room. Mr. Reed, a regular, orders a large latte with oat milk. Nora makes the latte quickly because the line is growing. She reaches for the milk jug on the left. It is regular milk, not oat milk. She pours, heats the milk, and puts the cup on the counter.

Mr. Reed takes a sip and smiles. "Hang tight, I think that is not oat milk." Nora feels heat on her cheeks. She stays calm. "Sorry about that," she says, and makes a new latte. She tells Bea later. Bea shrugs. "It happens to everyone." The line is long again. Nora takes a breath and says, "Okay, who's next?"

**Spanish:**

Lunes por la mañana, Nora se siente un poco atrasada. No durmió bien y la casa estaba fría. Hana lleva a Lila y Pia a la escuela hoy, así que Nora camina al café. Llega a las 6:45, se pone el delantal y dice: "¡Buenos días! ¡Vamos a empezar!" Es una frase que aprendió de Bea, y todavía suena graciosa en su propia boca.

A las 7:15 empieza la hora pico. La campana de la puerta suena rápido y el olor a café llena la sala. El señor Reed, un cliente habitual, pide un café con leche grande con leche de avena. Nora prepara el café con leche rápido porque la fila crece. Toma el envase de leche de la izquierda. Es leche normal, no leche de avena. Vierte, calienta la leche y pone la taza en el mostrador.

El señor Reed da un sorbo y sonríe. "Espere un momentico, creo que eso no es leche de avena." Nora siente calor en las mejillas. Se mantiene tranquila. "Disculpe," dice, y prepara otro café con leche. Le cuenta a Bea después. Bea encoge los hombros. "A todos nos pasa." La fila es larga otra vez. Nora respira hondo y dice: "Bien, ¿quién sigue?"

## Grammar

"Make" significa crear o preparar algo. En español, normalmente decimos "hacer" o "preparar." La forma en pasado es "made," no "mak-ed."

Pattern Practice:

1. I ___ a latte for the first customer of the day. (preparo)
2. Mr. Reed ___ a mistake with his order this morning. (equivocó)
3. I ___ breakfast for my daughter before I leave the house. (hago)

## Examples

1. Nora makes a big pot of coffee before she puts on her apron every morning.
2. Lila makes her bed on weekends, but she forgets on school days.
3. Hana says she will make lunch for the kids on Saturday.
4. Nora: "Bea, I need to make a new cup for Mr. Reed. The first one was wrong."
Bea: "Well, I can make you a quick one. Give me two minutes."
Nora: "Thanks. The line is already long."
Bea: "I know, I know. Hang tight, okay?"
Nora: "You always say that."
Bea: "Because it works."
5. Omar makes the soup fresh every day, and the whole café smells good.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. El cliente habitual pidió un café con leche grande con leche de avena.
2. Nora se siente un poco atrasada esta mañana porque no durmió bien.
3. La hora pico empezó a las 7:15 y la fila creció rápido.
4. Espere un momentico, por favor, que estoy ocupada.
5. Nora vierte la leche y la calienta para el café con leche.
6. El señor Reed da un sorbo y sonríe.

English → Spanish

1. The line is long, so I need to make the drinks fast.
2. She puts the cup on the counter and says, "Who's next?"

## Student Questions

1. What phrase does Nora say when she arrives at the café? (frase)
2. Why does Mr. Reed tell Nora the milk is wrong? (leche)
3. Do you walk to work or do you take the bus? (transporte)
4. How does Nora feel after she makes the wrong latte? (calma)

# TUESDAY

## Warmup

1. After work, I ___ the dishes in the kitchen. (hago)
2. The café is quiet today. It is a ___. (día tranquilo)
3. I ___ the cleaning when there are no customers. (hago)

## Vocabulary

1. dishes – los platos
2. cleaning – la limpieza
3. cash register – la caja registradora
4. no worries – no se preocupe / tranquilo
5. slow day – un día tranquilo

## Story

**English:**

Nora arrives at the café with her own coffee. Last night was long because of Lila's homework, and her eyes still feel heavy. The café is quiet, so Omar does the dishes and Nora does the cleaning.

Wren comes to the counter and says, "I made a mistake at the cash register. A customer paid twice." Nora says, "No worries. Tell Sam, and he will fix it." Sam fixes the problem in two minutes and makes a note in a small book: "Double payment, fixed." Then Nora calls out, "Who's up next?" The slow day feels almost nice.

**Spanish:**

Nora llega al café con su propio café. Anoche fue una noche larga por la tarea de Lila, y todavía se siente con los ojos pesados. El café está tranquilo, así que Omar lava los platos y Nora hace la limpieza.

Wren se acerca al mostrador y dice: "Hice un error en la caja registradora. Una clienta pagó dos veces." Nora dice: "No se preocupe. Dígale a Sam y él lo arregla." Sam arregla el problema en dos minutos y hace una nota en un librito: "Pago doble, arreglado." Luego Nora grita: "¿Quién sigue?" El día tranquilo casi se siente bien.

## Grammar

"Do" es para actividades y tareas: do the dishes, do the cleaning, do homework. "Make" es para crear algo: make coffee, make a mistake. La forma en pasado de "do" es "did," no "do-ed."

Pattern Practice:

1. Omar ___ the dishes after the morning rush. (hace)
2. I ___ my homework at night after the children sleep. (hago)
3. Nora ___ the cleaning on a slow day. (hizo)

## Examples

1. Omar does the dishes after lunch, and Nora does the cleaning while the café is quiet.
2. Wren does not know what to do when a customer pays twice, so she tells Sam.
3. Nora: "Wren, did you do the count on the cash register?"
Wren: "I did, but a customer paid twice. I don't know what to do."
Nora: "No worries. Tell Sam, and he will fix it."
Wren: "Thanks. I feel better now."

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Hice un error en la caja registradora. Una clienta pagó dos veces.
2. Omar lava los platos y Nora hace la limpieza.

English → Spanish

1. Sam makes a note in a small book: "Double payment, fixed."
2. The slow day feels almost nice.

## Student Questions

1. What does Wren tell Nora about the cash register? (error)
2. What does Nora do while the café is quiet? (limpieza)
3. Do you ever make a mistake at work? (equivocarse)

# WEDNESDAY

## Warmup

1. I ___ a list before I go to the grocery store. (hago)
2. I ___ the shopping on Wednesday after work. (hago)
3. I check my ___ after I pay at the store. (recibo)

## Vocabulary

1. ATM – cajero automático
2. groceries – la compra / los mandados
3. next in line / who's up next – el próximo / ¿quién sigue? (formal / casual)
4. receipt – recibo
5. poster board – cartulina

## Story

**English:**

After work, Nora stops at the ATM and then goes to the grocery store for groceries. Her phone rings, and Lila says, "I need poster board for my science project. It is due Friday." Nora adds it to her list.

The line is long, and her feet hurt under her shoes. The cashier calls out, "Next in line!" and then, "Who's up next?" Nora smiles because she says the same thing at the café. In the car, she checks her receipt: groceries, poster board, and she feels ready for Thursday.

**Spanish:**

Después del trabajo, Nora se detiene en el cajero automático y luego va al supermercado a hacer la compra. Suena su teléfono, y Lila dice: "Necesito cartulina para mi proyecto de ciencias. Es para el viernes." Nora la agrega a su lista.

La fila es larga y le duelen los pies bajo los zapatos. La cajera grita: "¡El próximo!" y luego, "¿Quién sigue?" Nora sonríe porque dice lo mismo en el café. En el carro, revisa su recibo: la compra, la cartulina, y se siente lista para el jueves.

## Grammar

"Make" y "do" se traducen como "hacer" en español, pero no son lo mismo. Con "make" decimos a list, a mistake, o a cup of coffee. Con "do" decimos the dishes, the cleaning, o homework. Ambos tienen formas irregulares en pasado: "made" y "did."

Pattern Practice:

1. Before I leave the house, I ___ a list of things to buy. (hago)
2. I ___ the dishes after dinner every night. (lavo)
3. She ___ a mistake at the cash register this morning. (equivocó)

## Examples

1. Nora makes a list at the grocery store, and she does not forget anything this time.
2. Lila does her science project at home, and she makes a big drawing for the poster.
3. Nora: "Did you do the math homework?"
Lila: "I did. But I need to make a poster for Friday."
Nora: "Okay, I will buy the poster board after work."
Lila: "Thanks, Mom. You are the best."

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. La cajera grita: "¡El próximo! ¿Quién sigue?"
2. Nora revisa su recibo: la compra, la cartulina, y se siente lista.

English → Spanish

1. I need to make a list of what to buy for the café before I go to the store.
2. She stops at the ATM and then goes to the grocery store.

## Student Questions

1. What does Lila need for her science project? (cartulina)
2. What does Nora check in the car after the grocery store? (recibo)
3. Do you use an ATM or do you pay by card? (cajero)

# THURSDAY

## Warmup

1. I ___ the order to table four after I make the coffee. (llevo)
2. The espresso machine is broken, so I ___ drip coffee instead. (preparo)
3. Can you ___ this plate to Omar in the kitchen? (lleva)

## Vocabulary

1. espresso machine – máquina de espresso
2. old-school – a la antigua
3. delivery – entrega
4. out of stock – agotado
5. back door – puerta trasera
6. a quick fix – una solución rápida

## Story

**English:**

Thursday morning, the espresso machine has a red light that goes on and off. There is no water in the machine, and it will not start. Nora sighs and says, "Okay, old-school today." She makes drip coffee in the big pot. The smell of plain coffee fills the kitchen.

Teo arrives at the back door with the bakery delivery. He takes the boxes to the kitchen and does not say much. Nora says, "Thanks, Teo," and he smiles. Then Bea tells Nora that the blueberry muffins are out of stock. Nora makes a small sign for the counter.

A customer asks, "Where is my latte?" Nora says, "The machine is down, but I can make you a drip coffee. It is a quick fix." The customer laughs and says, "Old-school is fine."

Nora thinks about her brother Luis, who lives in the family's home country. He and his wife have a nice house near their old town. He calls on Sundays, and she feels close to him. Sam walks by and says, "Good idea with the sign." Nora says okay and keeps working.

**Spanish:**

El jueves por la mañana, la máquina de espresso tiene una luz roja que se enciende y se apaga. No hay agua en la máquina y no funciona. Nora suspira y dice: "Bien, a la antigua hoy." Prepara café filtrado en la olla grande. El olor a café simple llena la cocina.

Teo llega por la puerta trasera con la entrega de la panadería. Lleva las cajas a la cocina y no dice mucho. Nora dice: "Gracias, Teo," y él sonríe. Luego Bea le dice a Nora que los muffins de arándanos están agotados. Nora hace una señal pequeña para el mostrador.

Una clienta pregunta: "¿Dónde está mi café con leche?" Nora dice: "La máquina está fuera de servicio, pero puedo prepararte un café filtrado. Es una solución rápida." La clienta ríe y dice: "A la antigua está bien."

Nora piensa en su hermano Luis, que vive en el país de la familia. Él y su esposa tienen una casa bonita cerca de su pueblo. Él llama los domingos, y ella se siente cerca de él. Sam pasa y dice: "Buena idea con la señal." Nora dice que sí y sigue trabajando.

## Grammar

"Take" significa llevar algo a otro lugar, alejándolo de ti. En español, es "llevar." "Bring" significa traer algo hacia ti, y en español es "traer." La forma en pasado de "take" es "took," no "tak-ed."

Pattern Practice:

1. Nora ___ the order to table four. (lleva)
2. Please ___ me a clean cup from the kitchen. (trae)
3. Teo ___ the boxes to the kitchen after the delivery. (llevó)

## Examples

1. Teo takes the bakery boxes to the kitchen, and Nora makes a note on the menu board.
2. Nora brings a napkin to the table, but she takes the empty cups back to the kitchen.
3. Nora takes the trash to the back of the café after the morning rush.
4. Nora: "Teo, can you take the boxes to the kitchen? I need to make a new sign."
Teo: "Sure. I will take them now."
Nora: "Thanks. And bring me the list of muffins we have left."
Teo: "Okay. I will bring it in two minutes."
Nora: "Perfect. The machine is down, so I will make drip coffee."
Teo: "Old-school is fine."
5. Sam does the paperwork at the counter, and Nora makes sure the kitchen is clean.
6. Lila takes her homework to school in the morning, and Nora makes breakfast before she leaves.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. La máquina de espresso está fuera de servicio, pero puedo prepararte un café filtrado.
2. Teo lleva las cajas a la cocina y no dice mucho.
3. Nora trae una servilleta y una toalla para la limpieza.
4. Los muffins de arándanos están agotados.
5. Nora hace una señal pequeña para el mostrador.
6. El tanque de agua está vacío y la bomba no arranca.

English → Spanish

1. I need to make a sign for the counter because the muffins are out of stock.
2. She brings the list of drinks to the kitchen and takes the empty cups away.

## Student Questions

# FRIDAY

## Warmup

1. I ___ a new hot chocolate for the little boy after he spills his cup. (preparo)
2. The boy ___ a drawing at the table with crayons. (hace)
3. I ___ the drawing to the counter and tape it on the wall. (llevo)

## Vocabulary

1. drawing – dibujo
2. to spill – derramar
3. napkin – servilleta
4. birthday cake – pastel de cumpleaños
5. we're going to take care of it – nosotros nos encargamos

## Story

**English:**

On Friday afternoon, a mother rushes in with her little boy, Max. He is four years old and will not stop moving. His mother orders one small cup of hot chocolate and a muffin. Nora makes the hot chocolate and takes it to the table. She gives Max some crayons, and he does a drawing at the table.

Then Max spills the cup. Chocolate goes on the table and on the floor. The mother looks down and feels embarrassed. Nora says in a calm voice, "We're going to take care of it." She brings a napkin and a towel and does the cleaning in one minute. Max does not cry. He just watches.

Max gives Nora his drawing. It is a big cup of chocolate, all wobbly and brown. Nora tapes it on the wall behind the counter. She feels proud. Then she makes Max a new hot chocolate, and the mother smiles. The little boy does not spill this one.

**Spanish:**

El viernes por la tarde, una mamá entra corriendo con su niño, Max. Tiene cuatro años y no para de moverse. Su mamá pide una tacita de chocolate caliente y un muffin. Nora prepara el chocolate y lo lleva a la mesa. Le da algunos crayones a Max, y él hace un dibujo en la mesa.

Luego Max derrama la taza. El chocolate se va a la mesa y al piso. La mamá mira abajo y se siente avergonzada. Nora dice con voz tranquila: "Nosotros nos encargamos." Trae una servilleta y una toalla y hace la limpieza en un minuto. Max no llora. Solo mira.

Max le da a Nora su dibujo. Es una taza grande de chocolate, todo torcido y marrón. Nora lo pega en la pared detrás del mostrador. Se siente orgullosa. Luego le prepara a Max un chocolate nuevo, y la mamá sonríe. El niño no derrama este.

## Grammar

"Make," "do," y "take" son los tres verbos irregulares de esta semana. "Make" es crear algo (made), "do" es una actividad (did), y "take" es llevar algo lejos de ti (took). Recuerden: "bring" es lo contrario de "take" — es traer algo hacia ti.

Pattern Practice:

1. A mother ___ her little boy to the park on Saturday. (lleva)
2. Nora ___ a small cup of hot chocolate for the boy. (preparó)
3. The boy ___ a drawing at the table. (hizo)

## Examples

1. Nora makes a fresh cup of hot chocolate, and Max drinks it without spilling a drop.
2. The mother takes Max to the table, and Nora does the cleaning in one minute.
3. Lila makes a drawing for her school project, and she takes it to class on Monday.
4. Nora: "Max, can you take the crayons back to the table?"
Max: "Okay. I will take them."
Nora: "Good. Now, do you want me to make you another hot chocolate?"
Max: "Yes! But I will not spill it."
Nora: "I'm sure you won't. Here you go."
Max: "Thank you, Nora!"
5. Hana takes the kids to the park, and Nora makes lunch for when they come home.
6. Bea does the count at the end of the day, and Nora makes sure the counter is clean.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Nora trae una servilleta y una toalla y hace la limpieza en un minuto.
2. El niño derrama el chocolate en la mesa y en el piso.
3. Max hace un dibujo en la mesa con los crayones.
4. Nora pega el dibujo en la pared detrás del mostrador.
5. La mamá se siente avergonzada cuando el niño derrama la taza.
6. Nosotros nos encargamos de todo, no se preocupe.

English → Spanish

1. I need to make a new hot chocolate for the customer. The old one is on the floor.
2. She takes the empty cups to the kitchen and does the cleaning.

## Student Questions

2. What happens to the hot chocolate? (derramar)
3. What does Nora use to clean the table? (servilleta)

# SATURDAY

## Warmup

## Vocabulary

1. two peas in a pod – dos gotas de agua
2. pancakes – panqueques
3. call in sick – reportarse enfermo / enferma
4. cover a shift – cubrir un turno
5. I already have plans – ya tengo planes

## Story

**English:**

Saturday morning is quiet. Nora stays under the warm blankets. Lila comes in, climbs into the bed, and says, "I made pancakes, but they are a little burned." Nora laughs. "We are two peas in a pod," she says.

Someone knocks on the door. It is Hana and Pia with a bag of fresh bread. Hana says, "Pia woke me up early, so we took a walk to the bakery." The bread smells warm and good. They plan a trip to the library at eleven.

Then the phone rings. It is Kit from the café. "Ivy called in sick for tomorrow. Can you cover her shift?" Nora knows Ivy often calls in sick on weekends. She takes a deep breath. "I'm sorry. I already have plans. Tomorrow is my day with Lila." She feels a little guilty, but she shakes it off. She covers for other people all the time.

At the library, Lila and Pia do a puzzle and read for an hour. By the end of the day, Nora feels calm and happy.

**Spanish:**

El sábado por la mañana está tranquilo. Nora se queda bajo las mantas calientes. Lila entra, se sube a la cama y dice: "Hice panqueques, pero están un poco quemados." Nora ríe. "Somos dos gotas de agua," dice.

Alguien toca la puerta. Son Hana y Pia con una bolsa de pan fresco. Hana dice: "Pia me despertó temprano, así que caminamos a la panadería." El pan huele caliente y rico. Planifican ir a la biblioteca a las once.

Luego suena el teléfono. Es Kit del café. "Ivy se reportó enferma para mañana. ¿Puedes cubrir su turno?" Nora sabe que Ivy a menudo se reporta enferma los fines de semana. Respira hondo. "Lo siento. Ya tengo planes. Mañana es mi día con Lila." Se siente un poco culpable, pero lo deja ir. Ella cubre por otros todo el tiempo.

En la biblioteca, Lila y Pia arman un rompecabezas y leen por una hora. Para el final del día, Nora se siente tranquila y feliz.

## Grammar

"Make," "do," y "take" son verbos irregulares. Sus formas en pasado son "made," "did," y "took." Usamos "take" para llevar algo lejos de nosotros, y "bring" para traer algo hacia nosotros.

Pattern Practice:

1. Nora ___ the girls to the library at eleven. (lleva)
2. Lila ___ pancakes this morning. (hizo)

## Examples

Nora: "I'm sorry. I already have plans. Tomorrow is my day with Lila."

# SUNDAY

## Warmup

1. I ___ coffee this morning on the porch. (preparo)
2. My daughter ___ her reading on the sofa while I rest. (hace)

## Vocabulary

1. regular – cliente habitual
2. dishes – los platos
3. espresso machine – máquina de espresso
4. call in sick – reportarse enfermo / enferma
5. a little behind – un poco atrasada

## Story

**English:**

Sunday morning, there is no alarm. Nora drinks coffee on the porch and remembers her week. On Monday, she made the wrong latte for Mr. Reed, a regular, and stayed calm. On Tuesday, Omar did the dishes, and on Thursday, the espresso machine was down, so she made drip coffee while Teo took the boxes to the kitchen.

On Friday, a little boy spilled his hot chocolate, and she did the cleaning. On Saturday, Ivy called in sick, and Nora said no. Lila brings her a second cup of coffee, and Nora feels calm. She chose her daughter, and it was the right choice.

**Spanish:**

El domingo por la mañana no hay alarma. Nora toma café en la terraza y recuerda su semana. El lunes, preparó el café con leche equivocado para el señor Reed, un cliente habitual, y se mantuvo tranquila. El martes, Omar lavó los platos, y el jueves, la máquina de espresso estaba fuera de servicio, así que preparó café filtrado mientras Teo llevaba las cajas a la cocina.

El viernes, un niño derramó su chocolate caliente, y ella hizo la limpieza. El sábado, Ivy se reportó enferma, y Nora dijo que no. Lila le trae a su mamá otra taza de café, y Nora se siente tranquila. Ella eligió a su hija, y fue la decisión correcta.

## Grammar

Esta semana usamos "make," "do," y "take" en tres tiempos: presente, pasado y futuro. Las formas en pasado son "made," "did," y "took." Para el futuro, usamos "will" más el verbo en forma base: "will make," "will do," "will take."

Pattern Practice:

1. Nora ___ drip coffee again if the machine breaks. (preparará)
2. Hana ___ the girls to school tomorrow. (llevará)

## Examples

1. Nora makes coffee on the porch and does not rush. She takes her time.
2. Lila makes a drawing and does her homework before Nora makes dinner.
3. Nora: "Lila, did you do your reading for school?"
Lila: "I did. I also made a list of new words."
Nora: "Good. Now, take a break. I will make tea."
Lila: "Thanks, Mom. Can you make it with honey?"
Nora: "Of course. Sit down, sweetie."

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

2. Nora toma café en la terraza y recuerda su semana.

English → Spanish
